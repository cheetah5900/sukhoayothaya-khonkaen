Thai Traditional Massage Course (150 Hours)

Published on 30 October 2026 | At Sukho Ayothaya Thai Traditional Massage School Khon Kaen

The 150-hour Thai Traditional Massage course (foundation Thai massage) at Sukho Ayothaya Thai Massage School Khon Kaen is for beginners who want a career in Thai massage. You learn the ten main energy lines (Sen Sib), acupressure points and standard stretching techniques, from zero to skilled.

Graduates receive a dual-language certificate from the school, which is under the supervision of the Ministry of Education.

The certificate can be used to register as a service provider in a health establishment with the Ministry of Public Health, according to the criteria and conditions set by law. The image above shows a sample certificate and a sample registration of our graduates.

Course fee: 7,900 THB. To enroll or ask about class dates, call Kru Nuch at 064-314-9999 or chat with us on LINE.
