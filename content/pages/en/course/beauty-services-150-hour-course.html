Beauty Services Course 150 Hours (Vietnamese Spa)

Published on 30 October 2026 | At Sukho Ayothaya Thai Traditional Massage School Khon Kaen

Discover the world of premium beauty and relaxation. Add in-demand beauty skills with the 150-hour Beauty Services course (Vietnamese Spa) at Sukho Ayothaya Thai Massage School Khon Kaen. Real lessons and real practice give you complete beauty and spa techniques, in a friendly class atmosphere that takes you from the basics to professional level.

This course is for anyone who wants to work in or open a spa or beauty business. Besides Vietnamese-style spa hair wash, you practise all 7 hands-on treatments:

1. Facial massage: various facial massage techniques
2. Skin care: cleansing, facial scrub, face pack and mask
3. Body spa & scrub: body scrub, body wrap and herbal steam
4. Hair removal: waxing
5. Nail care: hand and foot nail spa (paraffin treatment)
6. Head massage: Indian Head Massage
7. Cellulite massage

Featured treatment: Waxing

Turn your beauty skills into a career with waxing. Learn proper waxing methods: skin preparation, choosing professional wax (hard wax vs. soft wax), step-by-step hair removal on different body areas with minimal discomfort, and soothing aftercare.

✅ Professional tool handling and a hygienic waxing station
✅ Gentle, step-by-step techniques that reduce skin irritation
✅ Precautions, skin contraindications and aftercare
✅ Add a new service to your salon or spa menu right away

✅ Learn from expert instructors with real practice
✅ Hands-on focus and direct experience
✅ Ready to work in spa and beauty after graduating

Graduates receive a certificate and can register as a service provider in a health establishment, according to the criteria and conditions set by law.
