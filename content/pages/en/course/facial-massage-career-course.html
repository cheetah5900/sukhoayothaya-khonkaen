Professional Facial Massage Career Course

Published on 28 October 2026 | At Sukho Ayothaya Thai Traditional Massage School Khon Kaen

Facial massage is one of the highest-demand beauty and wellness services today. At Sukho Ayothaya Thai Massage School Khon Kaen, our Facial Massage Career Course provides comprehensive, one-on-one practical training designed to transform beauty enthusiasts into certified practitioners.

Students learn everything from skin anatomy, skin type identification, and professional product selection, to signature facial lifting massage strokes, acupressure techniques, and post-service skin rejuvenation. The course also covers jade stone facial massage; watch the sample clip in the gallery below.

Graduates receive a dual-language certificate certified by the Department of Health Service Support (HSS), Ministry of Public Health, fully qualifying them for employment in high-end spas, beauty salons, or starting their own wellness clinic.

"Mastering facial care techniques is a high-value skill that creates sustainable, lifelong income."
— Director Araya Thatburom (Kru Nuch)
