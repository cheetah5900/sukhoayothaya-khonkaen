Thai Traditional Medicine Assistant Course (330 Hours)

Published on 30 October 2026 | At Sukho Ayothaya Thai Traditional Massage School Khon Kaen

The 330-hour Thai Traditional Medicine Assistant course at Sukho Ayothaya Thai Massage School Khon Kaen is designed for those who want a serious career in Thai traditional medicine. You learn the fundamentals of Thai traditional medicine, pharmacy and therapeutic massage to work in clinics or hospitals.

Graduates receive a certificate from Sukho Ayothaya Thai Massage School Khon Kaen, a school under the supervision of the Ministry of Education.

After graduating, you can use the certificate to register as a service provider in a health establishment with the Ministry of Public Health, according to the categories and conditions set by law. The image above shows a graduate who has completed the course and registration.

Course fee: 19,000 THB. To enroll or ask about class dates, call Kru Nuch at 064-314-9999 or chat with us on LINE.
