Postpartum Health & Beauty Care Course (150 Hours)

Published on 30 October 2026 | At Sukho Ayothaya Thai Traditional Massage School Khon Kaen

The 150-hour Postpartum Health & Beauty Care course at Sukho Ayothaya Thai Massage School Khon Kaen teaches traditional Thai care for new mothers. It suits anyone who wants a career in postpartum care or to offer this service in a spa or massage shop.

Classes focus on real practice in a warm, clean classroom with all the equipment ready, such as an herbal steam tent, a salt pot compress set and a charcoal sitting stove. In a friendly atmosphere, students practise each treatment closely under the instructor's guidance: herbal compress massage, salt pot compress (thap mo kluea), belly steaming and charcoal sitting (nang than), to care for mothers after childbirth and restore their health.

✅ Herbal compress, steam and preparing the postpartum care set
✅ Salt pot compress, belly steaming and charcoal sitting
✅ Lactation (milk duct) massage
✅ Care to expel lochia and restore the uterus and muscles
✅ Learn the Thai herbs used for postpartum care and mix them yourself
✅ Theory and practice with close instructor guidance

Thai Herbs and Herbal Compress Making with Kru Nuch

Build your knowledge of Thai herbs and learn how to make herbal compress balls, taught by Kru Nuch, Thai traditional medicine practitioner Araya Thatburom, at Sukho Ayothaya Thai Massage School Khon Kaen.

If you are interested in Thai traditional wisdom, want to get to know Thai herbs and add herbal compress making to your skills, come and learn together with Kru Nuch.

Graduates receive a certificate and can register as a service provider in a health establishment, according to the criteria and conditions set by law.

Course fee: 15,000 THB. To enroll or ask about class dates, call Kru Nuch at 064-314-9999 or chat with us on LINE.
