Origin of Traditional Thai Medicine

“The second decade of Traditional Thai Medicine advancing into society and onto the global stage.” Initiated under the visionary leadership of distinguished advocates promoting ethics, expertise, and institutional standing, culminating in the historic enactment of the “Thai Traditional Medical Profession Act B.E. 2556 (2013)”.

History of Sukho Ayothaya

From its foundation as the Center for the Development of Traditional Thai Medicine, ‘Sukho Ayothaya’ upgraded to an officially authorized private vocational school: “Sukho Ayothaya Thai Traditional Massage School”, directed by Araya Thatburom (Kru Nuch), who possesses extensive professional experience training Thai massage therapists and spa therapists for luxury hotels and resorts domestically and abroad for more than a decade.

The core mission of Sukho Ayothaya is to empower individuals with sustainable careers, dignified livelihoods, and lifelong skills while disseminating timeless Thai wellness wisdom globally across 34 specialized vocational curriculums.

Quality Standards of Sukho Ayothaya Khon Kaen

  • •Officially licensed as a vocational school on March 28, 2019, by the Ministry of Education, with all curriculums officially approved.
  • •Fully accredited by the Department of Health Service Support (HSS), Ministry of Public Health, granting graduates legal practitioner registry across Thailand.
  • •Authorized by the Department of Skill Development, Ministry of Labour, as an official National Skill Standard Testing Center for Thai Massage Therapists Level 1 and Level 2.
