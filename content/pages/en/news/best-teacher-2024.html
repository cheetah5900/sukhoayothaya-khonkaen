🏆 National Honorary Award

Outstanding Vocational Teacher Award 2024

Published on 16 January 2024 | At Ministry of Education, Bangkok

So proud! Kru Nuch, Araya Thatburom of Sukho Ayothaya Massage School Khon Kaen was selected and honored with the prestigious "Outstanding Thai Massage and Spa Teacher Award at Best Thailand 2024"

This is another national award that confirms the quality of Sukho Ayothaya Massage School Khon Kaen as an institution truly committed to creating jobs and careers in Thai massage and spa. Many of our students now have careers and businesses, and have opened their own massage and spa shops in Thailand and abroad.

"The heart of a Thai massage teacher is patience and care for each student's body. We don't just teach students to memorize moves. We teach them to understand pressure, protect their own bodies, and pass relaxation on to customers the right way."
— Director Araya Thatburom (Kru Nuch)
