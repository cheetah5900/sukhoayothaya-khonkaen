🏆 National Business & Career Award

SMEs Career Creation Awards 2023

Published on 20 August 2023 | At Bangkok Convention Center

On 20 August 2023, Mr. Phakaynet Leng-ee, Assistant Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Commerce, chair and presenter of the awards at the 8th SMEs Career Creation Awards 2023 (20.08.2023), presented this award of pride to Kru Araya Thatburom (Kru Nuch), director of Sukho Ayothaya Thai Massage School Khon Kaen.

"This award is not just the success of Sukho Ayothaya. It belongs to every student we have helped gain a profession, open a shop and earn a steady, lasting income for their family."
— Director Araya Thatburom (Kru Nuch)

🎥 Video of the SMEs Career Creation Awards Ceremony

Sukho Ayothaya Thai Massage School Khon Kaen is committed to passing on Thai wisdom in Thai massage and health spa to international professional standards. The school is accredited by theDepartment of Health Service Support (HSS), Ministry of Public Health and theMinistry of Education , and is licensed as aNational Skill Standard Testing Center, Ministry of Labour for Thai Massage Therapist Levels 1 and 2 in the service sector, so graduates can legally register as service providers in health establishments.
