Vocational Star Institution Award

Published on 15 March 2024 | At Regional Education Office, Khon Kaen

The VOCATIONAL STARS 2025 stage brought together vocational students from across the country to show their potential in health and beauty, build skills for future careers, and reflect the spirit of development and goodwill from the ASEAN region.

The “THE 2nd HEALTH AND BEAUTY CONTEST VOCATIONAL STARS 2025” under the theme "Vocational education creates champions, toward professionals in health and beauty" was a lively event with vocational institutions from across the country, held to find model vocational students with all-round health and beauty skills. This year's contest focused on giving young people the chance to show their knowledge, skills and service mind in health, beauty and spa careers, combining Thai traditional knowledge with modern innovation to build skills for a quality job market.

🎥 Special Interview with Kru Nuch at VOCATIONAL STARS 2025

One of those honored on stage was Ms. Araya Thatburom, licensee of Sukho Ayothaya Thai Massage School Khon Kaen and winner of the "Outstanding Thai Massage and Spa Teacher" award at Best Thailand 2024, reflecting the dedication of Thai vocational teachers to help the new generation grow with quality and to share the beauty of Thai wisdom with the world.

"Competitions and national skill standard tests are key steps to building trust with employers and customers in Thailand and abroad. A standard certificate is not just paper. It is the seal of professionalism."
— Director Araya Thatburom, Sukho Ayothaya Thai Massage School Khon Kaen
