🏛️ Official Accreditation

Accredited National Skill Standard Testing Center

Published on 22 October 2024 | At Department of Skill Development, Ministry of Labour

The honor ceremony for education personnel and plaque presentation under the Office of the Private Education Commission (OPEC), held to encourage and support school administrators, teachers and students, at the Teachers' Council of Thailand auditorium, Ministry of Education.

At this prestigious ceremony, Thai traditional medicine practitioner Araya Thatburom, director of Sukho Ayothaya Thai Massage School Khon Kaen, received the plaque for "Outstanding School Administrator" for the 7th year in a row . It is another great source of pride for the organization and the Sukho Ayothaya Khon Kaen family.

"An award is an anchor... to behave well and act rightly in running education, and to teach students... to have quality... meet standards... and be good people... forever."
— Araya Thatburom (Kru Nuch)

The event was organized by the Office of the Private Education Commission (OPEC) together with the Non-Formal Private Education Association (Thailand) to honor individuals and schools with outstanding work that sets an example in developing the quality of Thai education.
