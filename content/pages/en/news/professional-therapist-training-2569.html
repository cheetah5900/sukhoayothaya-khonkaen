Professional Therapist Training for Spa and Thai Massage Businesses (31 Oct-1 Nov 2026)

Published on 31 Oct-1 Nov 2026 | At Sukho Ayothaya Thai Traditional Massage School Khon Kaen

Sukho Ayothaya Thai Massage School Khon Kaen is holding a Professional Therapist training made for spa and Thai massage businesses. It covers three main areas: Business System, Operation & Management (finance and team), and Marketing & Branding.

Day 1 (31 October 2026) at BAYASITA @ KKU hotel:
✅ The beauty service business playbook and writing a spa clinic business plan, by Dr. Suphalerk Phamornrattanapanya
✅ Managing a therapist team and building revenue, by Thai Traditional Medicine Practitioner Araya Thatburom
✅ Branding, digital marketing, sales strategy and building repeat customers, by Practitioner Pharinda Phanthurat
✅ Intensive workshop and Business Plan Clinic, by Practitioner Manas Saengkaew

Day 2 (1 November 2026): a study visit to see a massage and spa business at work at Sukho Ayothaya Massage & Spa: space layout, reception, booking, customer care and front-of-house management.
