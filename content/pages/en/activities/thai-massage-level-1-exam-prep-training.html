Published on 30 October 2026 | At Sukho Ayothaya Thai Traditional Massage School Khon Kaen

The atmosphere in the training room at Sukho Ayothaya Thai Massage School Khon Kaen reflects the professionalism and dedication of both the trainees and the instructors. ✅ Dedicated trainees: Trainees studied their handouts and textbooks closely and followed the lessons on the 10 main Sen lines and practice rules seriously.
✅ An environment built for learning: Handouts and teaching materials were well prepared and organized, reflecting the quality standard of Sukho Ayothaya Thai Massage School Khon Kaen's courses.
