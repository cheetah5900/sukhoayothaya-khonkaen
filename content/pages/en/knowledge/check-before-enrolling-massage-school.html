Stop and Check These 3 Things Before You Enroll in a Massage School

Published on 31 October 2026 | At Sukho Ayothaya Thai Traditional Massage School Khon Kaen

Before you enroll in a massage course, make sure the school has all three of these. Choosing the wrong school can cost you both money and time.

1. A valid license
The school must be properly licensed, so the certificate you receive can actually be used.

2. An accredited curriculum
The course must be accredited by the relevant authorities, such as the Ministry of Education and the Department of Health Service Support, Ministry of Public Health.

3. Real hands-on practice
You should practice real massage with a teacher guiding you, not only study theory.
