Want to Work in Massage and Spa? Do's and Don'ts

Published on 31 October 2026 | At Sukho Ayothaya Thai Traditional Massage School Khon Kaen

Before you decide to study massage for a career in massage and spa, Kru Nuch (Thai Traditional Medicine Practitioner Araya Thatburom) wants everyone to know what to do and what to avoid.

✅ Do
1. Choose a course that matches your goal, whether you want to work in a massage shop, a spa or open your own place
2. Study at an accredited school so your certificate is actually useful
3. Be ready for real hands-on practice, because massage takes practice to master
4. Be clear about why you are learning, so you choose the right course and plan your next step

❌ Don't
1. Choose a school only because it is cheap
2. Assume you can't learn without experience, when the course starts from the basics
3. Skip the practical part, which is the heart of massage
4. Enroll without reading the course details
