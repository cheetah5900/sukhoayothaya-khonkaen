A Career AI Can't Replace: Thai Massage Needs Your Hands and Skill

Published on 10 November 2026 | At Sukho Ayothaya Thai Traditional Massage School Khon Kaen

In an age when AI can take over many jobs, Thai massage is still a profession that needs "your hands and your skill". It is a discipline that requires understanding the energy lines, muscles and body of the person in front of you. The more you practise, the more expert you become.

✅ The right strength and pressure – the pressure that suits each person comes from practice and experience
✅ Reading each person's symptoms – the therapist must notice and understand where each person feels tight or sore
✅ Techniques that fit real bodies – adapting the massage to each client's body
